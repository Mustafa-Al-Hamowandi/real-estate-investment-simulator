Multi-Factor Depreciation Model

Each property is analyzed using a complex algorithm that considers:

  • Location Multiplier: Cities respond differently to each crisis type (e.g., SF hit harder in tech collapse)
  • Property Type Factor: Condos typically decline 25% more than houses during crises
  • Price Tier Impact: Luxury properties ($1M+) drop 40% faster than affordable housing
  • Size Volatility: Larger properties (3000+ sqft) show 15% higher volatility
  • Time Progression Curve: Crisis unfolds over months with acceleration, peak, and stabilization phases
Cascading Effects

The simulation models realistic market behavior:

  • Interest rate changes affect demand
  • Unemployment rises as crisis deepens
